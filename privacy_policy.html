Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Notepad is a local-first note taking application. Your notes are stored locally on your device by default.

Google Drive Backup

If you choose to use backup features, Notepad stores backup files only in your Google Drive App Data folder.

  • We cannot access your other Google Drive files.
  • Your backup data is not shared with third parties.
  • Only the Google Drive App Data scope is used.

AI Features

Voice formatting and AI-assisted editing features send only the text required to process your request to the configured AI provider.

Data Collection

Notepad does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or sell user data.
